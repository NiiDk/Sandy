## 2. Educator & Technical Review Items

The following safety and technique claims require qualified educator review or product manufacturer confirmation before marking questions as `ready`:

1. **Adhesive & Solvent Protocols (`frontal`):**
   - Minimum manufacturer recommended patch-test duration (typically 24–48 hours) for acrylic and silicone wig adhesives.
   - Recommended skin barrier/protector products for tropical climates (Accra / Ghanaian humidity and sweat resistance).
   - Safe protocol for emergency adhesive removal when acute skin irritation or burning is reported.
2. **Thermal Safety Thresholds (`curls`):**
   - Precise temperature ceiling recommendations for heat-friendly synthetic fibres (typically 120°C–140°C / 250°F–280°F vs human hair up to 180°C–200°C / 350°F–400°F).
   - Clarification that wet/damp fibre must never contact thermal irons to prevent irreversible cortex blistering.
3. **Wig Foundation & Weight Distribution (`chignon`):**
   - Guidelines on maximum veil weight supported by standard wig mesh vs natural hair.
   - Placement of U-pins to ensure pins weave through machine wefts rather than puncturing lace or irritating the client scalp.
